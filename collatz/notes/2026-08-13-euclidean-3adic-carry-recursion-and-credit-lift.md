# Euclidean 3-adic carry recursion and predecessor-credit lift

Date: 2026-08-13

Status: **exact algebraic recursion + finite level-46/73 diagnostics**. This note replaces flat correction-residue storage by a hierarchical 3-adic carry decomposition aligned with Euclidean macroblock concatenation. It does not prove Collatz.

## 1. Correction concatenation

For binary parity blocks `U,V` of lengths `L_U,L_V`, with odd counts `q_U,q_V` and corrections `R_U,R_V`, concatenation satisfies

\[
\boxed{
R_{UV}=3^{q_V}R_U+2^{L_U}R_V.
}
\]

The relative survival state composes independently by

\[
\Sigma_{UV}=\Sigma_U+\Sigma_V,
\qquad
M_{UV}=\min(M_U,\Sigma_U+M_V).
\]

Thus survival and correction arithmetic can be propagated simultaneously without expanding all internal parity words.

## 2. Hierarchical 3-adic digit split

Write

\[
R_V=\rho_V+3^{q_V}\kappa_V,
\qquad
0\le\rho_V<3^{q_V}.
\]

Also divide the shifted low residue by `3^{q_V}`:

\[
2^{L_U}\rho_V
=c\,3^{q_V}+\rho_0,
\qquad
0\le\rho_0<3^{q_V}.
\]

Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
R_{UV}
&=3^{q_V}R_U+2^{L_U}(\rho_V+3^{q_V}\kappa_V)\\
&=\rho_0+3^{q_V}
\left(R_U+2^{L_U}\kappa_V+c\right).
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
R_{UV}
=\rho_0+3^{q_V}Z,
\qquad
Z:=R_U+2^{L_U}\kappa_V+c.
}
\]

The low `q_V` ternary digits of the parent correction depend only on `rho_V`. The remaining high `q_U` ternary digits are obtained by dividing `Z` modulo `3^{q_U}`.

Therefore the flat state `R mod 3^{q_U+q_V}` can be replaced by an exact hierarchical carry state:

\[
\boxed{
(\rho_V,\kappa_V;R_U)
\longmapsto
(\rho_0,\ Z\bmod3^{q_U},\ \lfloor Z/3^{q_U}\rfloor).
}
\]

This is the 3-adic analogue of the previously derived low/high dyadic carry split.

## 3. Parent-residue collision condition

Consider two concatenated words `U_1V_1,U_2V_2` with the same child odd counts. Put

\[
D_U=R_{U_2}-R_{U_1},
\qquad
D_V=R_{V_2}-R_{V_1}.
\]

Then

\[
D=R_{U_2V_2}-R_{U_1V_1}
=3^{q_V}D_U+2^{L_U}D_V.
\]

For a full integer predecessor credit one needs

\[
3^{q_U+q_V}\mid D.
\]

Reduction modulo `3^{q_V}` first forces

\[
\boxed{3^{q_V}\mid D_V.}
\]

Write

\[
D_V=3^{q_V}\delta_V.
\]

Then the remaining condition is

\[
\boxed{
D_U+2^{L_U}\delta_V
\equiv0\pmod{3^{q_U}}.
}
\]

When it holds, the parent predecessor credit is exactly

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_{UV}
=\frac{D_U+2^{L_U}\delta_V}{3^{q_U}}.
}
\]

This formula is the exact cross-block credit-lift rule.

## 4. Prefix inheritance

If `V_1=V_2`, then `D_V=0`. If the two `U` words already satisfy

\[
D_U=3^{q_U}\Delta_U,
\]

then

\[
D=3^{q_U+q_V}\Delta_U.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{\Delta_{UV}=\Delta_U.}
\]

Any integer predecessor collision in a left macroblock survives unchanged under an identical future suffix. Credit fibres therefore form prefix-monotone cylinders.

## 5. Why the right block needs a carry match

A right-block credit `delta_V` does not automatically lift to the parent because it is multiplied by `2^{L_U}` rather than by `3^{q_U}`. It contributes only when the left correction difference supplies the exact complementary residue

\[
D_U\equiv-2^{L_U}\delta_V\pmod{3^{q_U}}.
\]

Thus new large credits are generated by a true two-channel interaction:

1. an integer-credit fibre in the right block;
2. a correction-difference class in the left block that cancels its 3-adic residue.

This explains why block order matters.

## 6. Level-46 diagnostic

Use the two previously certified Euclidean macroblocks

\[
U_{19}=1010110110101101101,
\]

with length `19` and mechanical odd count `12`, and

\[
U_{27}=011011011010110110101101101,
\]

with length `27` and mechanical odd count `17`.

The concatenation type

\[
\boxed{U_{46}=U_{19}U_{27}}
\]

has length `46` and mechanical odd count `29`.

Restrict to the exact partial one-slack fibre

\[
(\Sigma_U,M_U)=(0,0),
\qquad
(\Sigma_V,M_V)=(-1,-1).
\]

Thus

\[
q_U=12,
\qquad
q_V=16,
\qquad
q=28,
\]

and the full concatenation has

\[
(\Sigma,M)=(-1,-1).
\]

An exact difference-set search gives two orientations with full corrections

\[
R_1=73,753,304,060,593,
\]

\[
R_2=577,042,738,069,735.
\]

Their difference is

\[
\boxed{
R_2-R_1
=503,289,434,009,142
=22\cdot3^{28}.
}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{\Delta_{46}=22}
\]

is an exact integer predecessor credit in this partial fibre.

One explicit witness pair, written in time order, is

\[
\begin{aligned}
w_1&=1111111010101110000\,111111011101101010101000010,\\
w_2&=1011110010101101110\,001101011010110111001101110.
\end{aligned}
\]

They have the same length `46`, the same odd count `28`, and the same relative survival state `(-1,-1)` against the concatenated mechanical block.

The opposite block order `U_{27}U_{19}` was also checked. The strongest newly generated cross-collision in the analogous `(0,-1)` partial fibre has credit `17`; the inherited level-27 credit `19` therefore remains larger. This demonstrates that credit is a return-word-type/phase variable, not a function of block length alone.

## 7. Reusing the level-46 state at level 73

Treat the exact level-46 collision `Delta=22` as one right-block state rather than re-expanding its internal words.

Place a neutral `U_{27}` block on the left. Then

\[
L_U=27,
\qquad
q_U=17,
\qquad
\delta_V=22.
\]

Searching only the neutral correction classes of `U_{27}` for

\[
D_U\equiv-2^{27}\cdot22\pmod{3^{17}}
\]

gives an exact parent credit

\[
\boxed{\Delta_{73}=28.}
\]

The left-block correction extrema in the best class are

\[
R_{U,\mathrm{lo}}=294,434,803,
\qquad
R_{U,\mathrm{hi}}=957,569,351.
\]

No level-46 orientation enumeration is used in this step. Only the already-certified scalar collision state `delta_V=22` and the level-27 neutral correction classes enter.

Thus the fixed-one-slack credit ladder now contains

\[
\boxed{
1,2,3,5,11,19,22,28
}
\]

at the investigated Euclidean scales/types.

No monotonic or asymptotic theorem is inferred from these finite values.

## 8. Proof-program consequence

The useful state is no longer a flat table indexed by `R mod 3^q`. The exact Euclidean recursion naturally carries a nested 3-adic quotient/carry coordinate.

A proof-scale implementation should propagate, for each survival state and return-word type,

\[
\boxed{
(\Sigma,M,q;\ \rho,\kappa)
}
\]

or an exact aggregated equivalent sufficient to evaluate the cross-block congruence

\[
D_U+2^{L_U}\delta_V\equiv0\pmod{3^{q_U}}.
\]

The central asymptotic target is now sharper:

> show that along every sufficiently long critical-return orientation, the hierarchical Euclidean carry recursion generates an integer predecessor credit exceeding the available orbit headroom.

The finite level-46 and level-73 calculations demonstrate that credits can be propagated and increased without expanding the larger block's internal word set, but they do not prove unbounded credit growth or full coverage.
