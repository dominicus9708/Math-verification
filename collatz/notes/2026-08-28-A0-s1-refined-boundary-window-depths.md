## 7. DSD channel-separation warning

The newly forced first-75 Christoffel defect must **not** be combined naively with the terminal ternary windows.

The defect generated by movements among the first 75 parity positions is an early-ordinal phenomenon. In the integer correction defect

\[
\mathcal E=C_{\rm th}-C,
\]

each such early odd-ordinal term carries a very large power of `3`, because its coefficient contains `3^{j_0-r}` with `r\le75` while

\[
j_0=65868186701.
\]

Therefore these early defect terms vanish modulo `3^28` and even modulo `3^47`.

Consequently:

\[
\boxed{

\not\Rightarrow
\text{terminal-}3\text{-adic contradiction}.
}
\]

This is consistent with the previously certified fact that terminal ballot projections saturate all 3-adic units through 47 trits.

The same early defect is, however, visible in the dyadic / formation-address channel through its first displaced position. That asymmetry is audited separately in the companion early-defect channel-visibility certificate.
