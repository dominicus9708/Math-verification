# A0 s=1: 72-bit closure and near-threshold block-jump

Date: 2026-08-28

Status: **SAFE finite closure / SAFE new necessary condition / OPEN full bridge.**

This step continues the target-aware valuation program after the modular prefix decoder.
The key observation is that the 72-bit physical shell turns the 2-adic parity address from a congruence class into an ordinary integer once the prefix depth reaches 72.

## 1. Universal parity address

For a parity word `w` of length `h` with odd positions

\[
0\le a_1<a_2<\cdots<a_q<h,
\]

define

\[
A_h(w)
=
-\sum_{r=1}^{q}3^{-r}2^{a_r}
\pmod{2^h}.
\]

This is the start residue corresponding to the parity prefix.  Equivalently,

\[
X\equiv A_h(w)\pmod{2^h}
\]

if and only if `w` is the first `h` parity symbols of `X` under

\[
T(x)=
\begin{cases}
(3x+1)/2,&x\text{ odd},\\
x/2,&x\text{ even}.
\end{cases}
\]

For a physical candidate satisfying

\[
0<X<2^{72},
\]

any prefix depth `h>=72` therefore satisfies the stronger condition

\[
\boxed{A_h(w)=X}
\]

as an ordinary integer.  No high address bit remains free after depth 72.

This is the first exact block-jump mechanism: a long proposed parity block can be rejected by its address lift without iterating every internal correction atom.

## 2. Exact threshold branch closes at depth 75

Let `W_th` be the exact lower-ballot threshold word

\[
Q_{\rm th}(n)=\lceil n\log_3 2\rceil.
\]

Its first 72 bits have address

\[
\boxed{
A_{72}(W_{\rm th})
=4\,697\,939\,311\,072\,332\,635\,131.
}
\]

This lies in the strict 72-bit shell.

The next two lifts remain unchanged:

\[
A_{73}=A_{74}=A_{72}.
\]

At depth 75, however,

\[
\boxed{
A_{75}=A_{72}+2^{74}
=23\,587\,405\,242\,550\,913\,489\,915.
}
\]

Hence

\[
A_{75}>2^{72}.
\]

Therefore no integer in the physical shell can follow the exact threshold word through all first 75 positions.

The direct Collatz orbit of the unique 72-bit threshold address gives the same result independently: it agrees with the threshold through zero-indexed positions `0..73` and first disagrees at position `74`.

Thus the exact-threshold, zero-deviation branch is closed without materializing the enormous `t0` correction.

## 3. Near-threshold finite shell

To go beyond the exact threshold branch, define the first-75 Hamming defect

\[
d_{75}(w)=d_H\bigl(w_{[0,75)},W_{{\rm th},[0,75)}\bigr).
\]

All pure-ballot words with

\[
d_{75}\le4
\]

can be exhaustively generated by the surplus process

\[
S_n=Q_w(n)-Q_{\rm th}(n)\ge0.
\]

At a threshold `0`, a flip creates `+1` surplus.  At a threshold `1`, a flip consumes one unit and is allowed only when the surplus remains nonnegative.

The exact counts by Hamming distance are:

| distance | ballot words | strict physical shell | also `X<=X_max` | latest first ballot failure |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | — |
| 1 | 27 | 1 | 1 | 88 |
| 2 | 987 | 62 | 18 | 110 |
| 3 | 14,003 | 916 | 386 | 161 |
| 4 | 248,564 | 15,560 | 6,174 | 222 |

Totals:

\[
263\,582
\]

pure-ballot words occur within radius four.

Of them,

\[
16\,539
\]

have an address in

\[
2^{71}<X<2^{72}.
\]

Using the already certified necessary bound

\[
X\le
3\,295\,414\,002\,074\,039\,191\,016,
\]

only

\[
\boxed{6\,579}
\]

remain.

Every one of those 6,579 exact integers is then extended by the actual Collatz map.  Every trajectory violates the pure-ballot condition by prefix 222; none survives the local scan.

Consequently any full A0 s=1 survivor satisfying the earlier physical and correction-envelope conditions must obey

\[
\boxed{
d_{75}\ge5.
}
\]

This is a new deterministic necessary condition.

## 4. Relation to the Christoffel DAG

The previously certified Christoffel/Stern-Brocot DAG remains useful as the compressed representation of the threshold skeleton.

The new result clarifies its role.  The DAG need not enumerate every admissible deviation.  Instead:

1. the DAG supplies the exact threshold block and correction transfers;
2. the parity-address map converts a proposed prefix into its unique 2-adic start residue;
3. the 72-bit physical shell closes the Hensel lift after 72 address bits;
4. deviations from the DAG skeleton are then explored only in a bounded defect shell;
5. each surviving physical address is continued deterministically rather than branching again.

Thus the current block-jump architecture is

\[
\boxed{
\text{Christoffel skeleton}
\to
\text{bounded deviation shell}
\to
\text{72-bit address closure}
\to
\text{deterministic orbit extension}.
}
\]

## 5. DSD audit

### SAFE

- the universal 2-adic parity-address formula;
- after depth 72, a physical address has no free higher lift bits;
- exact threshold address at depths 72, 73, 74, and 75;
- rejection of the exact threshold branch by depth 75;
- exhaustive generation of every pure-ballot first-75 word at Hamming distance at most four;
- exact counts `263,582`, `16,539`, and `6,579`;
- deterministic continuation of all 6,579 bounded physical addresses;
- failure of all of them by prefix 222;
- the necessary condition
  \[
  d_{75}\ge5.
  \]

### NOT PROMOTED

- Hamming distance is not identified with the previously used ordered-position defect or Hensel min-plus cost;
- the radius-four closure is not extrapolated statistically to radius five or beyond;
- the 6,579 trajectories are not treated as a random sample;
- pure-ballot rejection is not replaced by, or confused with, `C4F`;
- no claim is made that all A0 s=1 candidates are closed.

### OPEN

The next useful compression is to replace raw Hamming radius by a defect coordinate that composes on Christoffel DAG nodes.  Such a state should preserve at minimum

\[
(\text{node},\ r,\ S,\ D,\ A\bmod2^{72})
\]

or a rigorously smaller sufficient quotient, where `r` is decoded odd rank, `S` is ballot surplus, `D` is a certified deviation/defect budget, and `A` is the physical parity address.

A state merge is permitted only if every future valuation and every `C4F`-relevant predicate is invariant under the merge.
