## 10. What this does not close

The following remain open and must not be conflated with the theorem above.

1. **Different-q alternate pre-gate paths.**  The alternate smaller predecessor may have an even count different from 13.  Such a relation is not represented by the same-q integer-credit state used here.
2. **G13 relation fibres outside the survival-conditioned transition section.**  The present parent-credit envelope comes from that identified section only.
3. **E=13 itself.**  The theorem blocks one predecessor-construction mechanism; it does not prove that an unresolved E=13 actual path cannot exist.
4. **R1 and Collatz globally.**  Neither is closed by this result alone.
