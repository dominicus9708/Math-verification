## 1. Purpose

MATH-187 writes a common one-step recurrence carrying both

- global depth / odd-count coordinates `(k,q)`, and
- exact affine-cylinder exponents `(H,Q)`.

Those pairs were deliberately kept distinct because composed macro states may be represented from different origins.

For the intended single `r=1..21`, `k=2..41` executor, however, one may choose a **synchronized ordinary-source origin** and keep every channel referenced to that same source.

Under that explicit synchronization invariant, two coordinates become exactly redundant.

MATH-187 gives

\qquad

\[

No asymptotic or finite-depth assumption is involved.

## 7. Reduced one-step transition

Let

\[
\delta_q=m(q+1)-m(q),
\qquad
\rho=2^k/3^q,
\qquad
\Omega=2^{q+m(q)}/3^q,
\]

and let `b` be the actual shortcut parity.

Then

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\eta&=(b-B)\bmod2,\\
M'&=\left\lfloor\frac{M+1-\eta}{2}\right\rfloor,\\
k'&=k+1,\\
q'&=q+b,\\
\Sigma'&=\Sigma+(1-b)\rho,\\

The branch is empty if `M'=0`.

The remaining derived slack after the step is

\[
\boxed{
