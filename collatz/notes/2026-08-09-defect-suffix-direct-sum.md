# Collision-free defect / suffix direct-sum factorization

Date: 2026-08-09

Status: **DERIVED EXACT FACTORIZATION + INDEPENDENT SMALL CHECK**

This note records a stronger consequence of the high-resolution defect coordinate.  Inside a fixed coefficient-surviving prefix cell, changing the prefix by a defect translates every common future suffix by the same target-resolution residue.  The prefix-defect and future-suffix coordinates therefore form a collision-free modular direct sum.

This is a structural reformulation of the parity/residue bijection, not a global Collatz proof.

## 1. Prefix cell and future suffix

Fix prefix depth `h`, odd-count `q`, and target depth

\[
K=h+m.
\]

Put

\[
M=2^h,\qquad Q=2^K,\qquad P=3^q.
\]

Let `w` be a coefficient-surviving prefix in a fixed `(h,q)` cell, with correction

\[
R(w).
\]

Choose a reference prefix `w*` in the same cell, with correction `R*`.

Let `v` be any length-`m` future parity suffix that is coefficient-admissible after depth `h` with accumulated odd-count `q`.  Write its affine map as

\[
T^m(x)=\frac{3^{s(v)}x+S(v)}{2^m}.
\]

Then concatenation gives

\[
T^K(n)
=
\frac{
3^{s(v)}\bigl(Pn+R(w)\bigr)+M S(v)
}{Q}.
\]

## 2. Suffix-independent prefix translation

Let

\[
r_K(wv)
\]

be the canonical starting residue modulo `Q` of the full parity word `wv`.

The full correction difference between `w*v` and `wv` is

\[
3^{s(v)}(R^*-R(w)).
\]

The total odd multiplier is

\[
P3^{s(v)}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\begin{aligned}
r_K(wv)-r_K(w^*v)
&\equiv
(P3^{s(v)})^{-1}3^{s(v)}(R^*-R(w))\\
&\equiv
P^{-1}(R^*-R(w))
\pmod Q.
\end{aligned}
\]

Define the target-resolution defect translation

\[
\boxed{
A_K(w)
=[P^{-1}(R^*-R(w))]_{2^K}.
}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
r_K(wv)=[r_K(w^*v)+A_K(w)]_{2^K}.}
\]

Crucially, `A_K(w)` is independent of the future suffix `v`.

This is the global target-resolution meaning of the high-resolution defect coordinate from `high-resolution-defect-cylinder.md`.

## 3. Direct-sum sets

Let

\[
\mathcal A
=\{A_K(w):w\text{ in the fixed prefix cell}\},
\]

and let

\[
\mathcal B
=\{r_K(w^*v):v\text{ an admissible future suffix}\}.
\]

The complete target-depth canonical residue set generated by this prefix cell is

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{cell},K}
=\mathcal A+\mathcal B
\pmod{2^K}.
}
\]

Moreover the sum representation is unique.

Indeed, suppose

\[
A_K(w_1)+r_K(w^*v_1)
\equiv
A_K(w_2)+r_K(w^*v_2)
\pmod{2^K}.
\]

Then

\[
r_K(w_1v_1)=r_K(w_2v_2).
\]

The length-`K` parity-vector / residue bijection implies

\[
w_1v_1=w_2v_2.
\]

Since the split position is fixed,

\[
w_1=w_2,
\qquad
v_1=v_2.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
|\mathcal R_{\mathrm{cell},K}|
=|\mathcal A|\,|\mathcal B|.
}
\]

In additive-combinatorial notation the sum is a modular direct sum on these finite sets.

## 4. Difference-set consequence

Unique representation implies a simple anti-collision statement.

If

\[
a_1-a_2\equiv b_2-b_1\pmod{2^K},
\]

with `a_i in A` and `b_i in B`, then

\[
a_1+b_1\equiv a_2+b_2.
\]

Uniqueness forces

\[
a_1=a_2,
\qquad
b_1=b_2.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
(\mathcal A-\mathcal A)
\cap
(\mathcal B-\mathcal B)
=\{0\}
\quad\text{mod }2^K.
}
\]

This is an exact no-collision form of prefix/suffix anti-alignment.  It is weaker than the quantitative small-residue separation needed for a proof, but it identifies the required strengthening: not merely prevent exact cancellation, but bound how close the modular sum can lie to the ordinary interval near zero.

## 5. Generating-function factorization

Define group-algebra generating functions

\[
F_{\mathcal A}(X)=\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}X^a,
\]

\[
F_{\mathcal B}(X)=\sum_{b\in\mathcal B}X^b
\]

in

\[
\mathbb Z[X]/(X^{2^K}-1).
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
F_{\mathrm{cell},K}(X)
=F_{\mathcal A}(X)F_{\mathcal B}(X)
\pmod{X^{2^K}-1}.
}

Because the sum representation is unique, every coefficient of this product is `0` or `1`; there are no multiplicity collisions.

This is the exact static-aggregation factorization of the prefix defect term and the future suffix term.

## 6. Exact Fourier factorization

For every frequency `t mod 2^K`, evaluate at

\[
\omega=e^{2\pi i/2^K}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{r\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{cell},K}}\omega^{tr}
=\left(\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}\omega^{ta}\right)
 \left(\sum_{b\in\mathcal B}\omega^{tb}\right).
}
\]

After normalization by cardinality,

\[
\boxed{
\widehat\nu_{\mathrm{cell}}(t)
=\widehat\nu_{\mathcal A}(t)
 \widehat\nu_{\mathcal B}(t).
}
\]

Thus any rigorous Fourier attenuation obtained on either the defect side or the suffix side multiplies exactly at cell level.  As noted elsewhere in the repository, ordinary square-root-scale Fourier decay alone is still insufficient to certify absence of one exceptional small residue; the exact interval/min-plus layer remains necessary for final pruning.

## 7. Relation to low/high bit cylinders

All elements of `B` reduce modulo `2^h` to the same reference prefix residue `r*`, while reduction of `A` modulo `2^h` records which prefix word was chosen.

Therefore the direct-sum structure is hierarchical in binary digits:

1. low `h` bits identify the prefix defect class;
2. conditioned on that prefix, the remaining high `m` bits identify the future suffix lift.

The `A=U+2^hW` decomposition in the previous note is exactly the carry-adjusted form of this hierarchy.

This also explains why the direct sum is collision-free without requiring a probabilistic independence assumption.

## 8. Independent finite check

Wolfram exact enumeration checked every coefficient-admissible fixed prefix cell with

\[
1\le h\le6,
\qquad
1\le m\le4.
\]

Across

\[
\boxed{44}
\]

`(h,q,m)` cases it verified simultaneously that:

- every direct full canonical residue equals `A_K(w)+r_K(w*v) mod 2^K`;
- the pairwise sum list has no duplicates;
- the sum-set cardinality equals the product of prefix-cell and admissible-suffix cardinalities.

All checks used exact modular/integer arithmetic.

## 9. Proof-program consequence

The target is now an additive small-ball problem with exact product structure:

\[
\boxed{
(\mathcal A+\mathcal B)
\cap[1,X-1]
=\varnothing
}

for the dangerous prefix cells and a growing threshold `X`.

The exact direct-sum property already rules out modular collisions; a global proof requires a quantitative strengthening that keeps the sumset away from a growing ordinary interval near zero.

The available tools now attack the two factors separately:

- sparse defect transfer for `A`;
- exact E/O Bellman / interval recursion for `B`;
- exact rectangle/cylinder certification for their interaction;
- Fourier factorization as an optional analytic filter, with exact interval counts as the final certificate.
