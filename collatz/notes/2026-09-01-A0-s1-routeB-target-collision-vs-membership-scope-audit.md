1. **Target-collision / observation coordinates**

     SAFE physical upper bound on \(X\).

These are related but not interchangeable.

## 1. Why first-defect shells do not vanish merely from dyadic inequality

The exact 14-root forest says that every remaining physical shell has first
threshold disagreement

\[
f\in\{2,5,8,10,13,16,18,21,24,27,29,32,35,37\}.
\]

For the target-relative dominance decoder, this indeed means sufficiently deep
dyadic target-equality observation separates every such word from the target.

However the prefix-defect pruning certificate explicitly states that surviving
cylinders are **not** required to have correction equal to the threshold.  A
positive correction loss is allowed; it instead contributes to

\[
\eta=(C_{th}-C(W))/3^{j_0}
\]

and yields a physical upper bound through the real envelope.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
C(W)\not\equiv C_{th}\pmod{2^K}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{membership rejection}
}
\]

unless an independently defined gate actually requires that equality.

## 2. Legal use of the target-collision machinery

The target-relative adic decoder remains useful for:

- identifying which structural deviations are visible at a chosen resolution;
- localizing the first/last relevant one-position displacements;
- building projective H/L/slack coordinates;
- deriving forced parity and carry constraints;
- proving positive lower bounds on the actual normalized defect generated by a
  constrained deviation.

The last item is the bridge to physical pruning.

For example, the ternary-collider defect-gap theorem proves that a candidate
which differs within the observed last-L one positions while retaining the
ternary collision pays at least

\[
\eta_{suffix}\ge1/8.
\]

That is a legal membership-relevant conclusion because \(\eta\) is the same
monotone defect used by the real-envelope certificate.

## 3. Updated A/B/C interpretation

### A — physical closure

A family closes when an exact lower bound \(\underline\eta\) implies

\[
X_{\min}(\text{family})
>
X_{\max}(\underline\eta)
\]

under the certified real envelope, or another separately defined membership
condition fails.

### B — structural/state reuse

Adic, ballot, H/L, hierarchy, slack, and displacement states may be quotiented
or reused when their exact right-congruence / projective theorem applies.

### C — source refinement

The exact channel interval is refined, with the already certified strict
cardinality descent on nonsingletons.

Target-collision mismatch by itself belongs to B/observation unless the active
A-gate explicitly requires target equality.

## 4. Next proof interface

The useful composition is now

\[
\boxed{
\text{projective adic constraints}
\to

\text{physical }X\text{ pruning}

\[

\[
\eta(UV)
=

\]

so lower defect bounds can be transported through the same H/L product grammar
without singleton expansion.
