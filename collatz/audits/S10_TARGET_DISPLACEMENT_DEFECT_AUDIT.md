## N — Non-independence

This is the central audit result.

If `B_prefix` is a historical lower bound on the same already-realized defect, then

\[
B_{prefix}\le N
\]

under the matching normalization/hypotheses.

It is invalid to form

\[
N+B_{prefix}
\]

and treat that as a stronger physical defect.  That counts the same realized displacement twice.

Likewise, the exact one-displacement decomposition, phase-slack representation, first-crossing skew collapse, and specialized branch defect floors are not additive solely because they use different coordinates.

Only a theorem certifying a genuinely unresolved future contribution can supply an additive strengthening after exact transport.

**Status: duplicate-prefix addition REJECTED.**
